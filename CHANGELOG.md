# Changelog

## 0.2.0

The first public release: tidy a big library, and Shelf on the Mac.

### New

- **Details from the web.** In the editor, *Find details online* looks the book up on Open Library (by ISBN, or title and author) and shows what it found field by field; tick what to take. For many books at once, select them and choose *Fill in missing details*: only empty fields and missing covers are filled in.
- **Bulk editing.** In the list view, Ctrl/Cmd-click, Shift-click or Ctrl/Cmd+A to select books, then set the author or series (numbered in list order), add or remove tags and collections, set a rating, or send them all to your e-reader.
- **Duplicate finder.** *Find duplicates* in the Library finds books you have more than one copy of (same ISBN, or the same title and author) and merges them, keeping every copy's tags, collections, reading progress and details.
- **macOS.** Shelf now builds for Macs with Apple Silicon and Intel. It's new there: please report anything that doesn't work.
- **A proper table.** The list view sorts by any column (click again to reverse), lets you choose its columns, and has compact rows and type-to-find.
- **Updates.** Shelf checks for new versions and offers them in a popup; Settings → About Shelf shows the version and checks on demand. Updates are signed.
- **A sidebar you can collapse** to icons (Ctrl/Cmd+B).
- **Goodreads export import.** Goodreads retired the shelf feeds Shelf read in November 2026, so Accounts now walks you through importing your Goodreads library export instead.

### Better

- Smoother scrolling in every view, and a sidebar that no longer stutters.
- The top bar fades content away softly instead of showing a hard strip.
- The edit window has a full-window backdrop, a solid form, and a scrollbar clear of its corners.
- A new bookcase icon.
